1. Introduction and Effective Date

This Privacy Policy applies to EngiCalc Pro ("the Application"), developed by Krishna Patil Rajput ("the Developer"). This policy is effective as of March 30, 2026 and explains how data is handled when you use EngiCalc Pro.

2. Information the App Processes

EngiCalc Pro is designed primarily as a client-side offline mathematical, statistical, and engineering calculator tool. All user-entered mathematical expressions, dataset numbers, matrices, unit conversion inputs, statistical values, and networking inputs are processed locally on your device. The Application does not transmit, store on external developer servers, or sell your mathematical data or calculation inputs.

3. Google AdMob and Advertising

EngiCalc Pro integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve advertisements. Depending on device settings and applicable privacy choices, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain device information independently for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Data processed by Google AdMob SDK may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) or device identifiers.
  • Coarse location data derived from IP addresses.
  • Ad interaction metrics and diagnostic performance reports.

For more information about how Google collects and uses data, please review:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • Google's Advertising Information

4. Local Storage and Calculation History

The Application stores calculation history and user preferences (such as Dark Mode theme and default Angle Units) strictly on your device using Android's local Room Database and DataStore Preferences. This data remains under your direct control on your device.

5. App Permissions

The Application requests standard Android permissions necessary for advertisement delivery and external developer links:

  • INTERNET: Required for fetching AdMob advertisements and launching public developer links when requested by the user.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify active internet connectivity before requesting advertisement content.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Except for automated processing performed independently by Google AdMob as disclosed in Section 3, the Application does not share, rent, or trade user data with third-party companies or services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all calculation history and application preferences are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over data deletion:

  • You can clear your calculation history at any time through the Application settings.
  • Uninstalling the Application automatically removes all locally stored history and preferences from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

EngiCalc Pro is an educational, scientific, and engineering calculator intended for students, engineers, and professionals aged 13 and older. The Application does not knowingly collect or target personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age, in compliance with Google Play's Target Audience and Content policies.

9. Data Security

We prioritize user security. Since user calculation data and mathematical inputs remain localized on your Android device, the risk of external server-side data breaches regarding your mathematical computations is minimized.
